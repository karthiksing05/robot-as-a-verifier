# LP-40 Thermal Label Printer — User Manual

**Read this page before first use.** The LP-40 prints on 40 mm direct-thermal label rolls. No ink or ribbon is needed.

## Parts
1. Roll cover, with a lip at its front edge 2. Roll bay and spindle 3. Label exit slot 4. Display 5. Status light 6. POWER switch 7. FEED 8. PRINT 9. DARKNESS dial

## Power
Connect the USB cable to a USB port. Flip **POWER** up to ON. The status light glows white and the display shows the darkness setting and the label count, for example `DARK 5  000`. Flip it down to switch off. The label count starts again at 000 each time the printer is switched on.

## Loading a roll
1. Put a fingertip under the lip at the front of the roll cover and lift. The cover swings up and stays open on its own.
2. Drop the roll onto the spindle with the labels coming off the bottom, toward the front.
3. Pull the end of the liner past the exit slot.
4. Lower the cover. It closes under its own weight.

## Setting darkness
- Turn **DARKNESS** clockwise for darker print and counterclockwise for lighter, from 1 (lightest) to 9 (darkest). Each setting is one step of the dial's sweep.
- The display shows the setting after `DARK`.

## Feeding and printing
- Press **FEED** to advance one blank label. The display shows `FEED` for about a second.
- Press **PRINT** to print one label. The status light turns blue and the display shows `PRINTING` for about 3 seconds. When the label is out, the light returns to white and the label count goes up by one.
- Do not open the cover while a label is printing.
- With the cover open, FEED and PRINT do nothing. The display shows `COVER` and the status light turns red.

## Display messages
| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `DARK n  ccc` | Ready. Darkness n (1 to 9), ccc labels printed since power-on | Nothing |
| `FEED` | Advancing one blank label | Wait |
| `PRINTING` | Printing a label | Wait for the light to turn white |
| `COVER` | The roll cover is open | Close the cover. The display returns to `DARK` |

## Care
Use only 40 mm direct-thermal labels. Keep the printer dry and away from heat. Wipe with a dry cloth. Do not pull labels out while printing.
