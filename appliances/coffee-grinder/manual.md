# BG-12 Burr Coffee Grinder — User Manual

**Read this page before first use.** The BG-12 is a compact countertop burr grinder with a charcoal body and a smoked bean hopper.

## Parts
1. Hopper lid (smoked, with a front lip) 2. Bean hopper 3. Display 4. POWER switch 5. GRIND dial 6. START button 7. TIME dial 8. Grounds bin

## Power
Connect the USB cable to a USB port. Flip **POWER** up to ON. The display shows the grind setting and the grind time, for example `GRIND 1 5s`. Flip it down to switch off. The display goes blank.

## Filling the hopper
1. Lift the hopper lid by its front lip until it rests open.
2. Pour in whole beans. Do not fill above the rim.
3. Close the lid. It closes under its own weight.

The grinder will not start while the lid is open.

## Setting the grind
- Turn **GRIND** clockwise for a coarser grind: settings 1 (finest, espresso) to 12 (coarsest, French press), one setting at a time. The display shows the setting after `GRIND`.
- Turn **TIME** clockwise for a longer grind: 5 to 30 seconds, in steps of 5 seconds. The display shows the time.
- Both dials stay where you leave them.

## Grinding
1. Close the lid.
2. Press **START**. The display shows `GRINDING` and counts down the seconds left.
3. To stop early, press **START** again. Opening the lid also stops the grinder at once.
4. When the time is up the display shows `DONE`. Empty the grounds bin.

## Display messages
| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `GRIND 4 15s` | Ready: grind setting and time | Press START to grind |
| `GRINDING 12s` | Grinding: seconds left | Wait, or press START to stop |
| `DONE` | The grind has finished | Empty the bin. Press START to grind again |
| `LID` | The hopper lid is open | Close the lid, then press START |

## Care
Unplug before cleaning. Brush the burrs and the bin with a dry brush. Do not wash the body or put liquids in the hopper. Grind only roasted coffee beans.
