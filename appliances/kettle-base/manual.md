# KB-1 Temperature Kettle — User Manual

**Model KB-1. Read this page before first use.**

## Parts
1. Kettle jug 2. Lid (hinged at the back) 3. Lid lip 4. Jug handle 5. Power base with heater ring light 6. Display 7. WARM button 8. BOIL button 9. TEMP dial 10. POWER switch

The control panel is the sloped face at the front of the base. From left to right it holds **WARM**, **BOIL**, **POWER** and **TEMP**. The display is on the front of the base, below the panel.

## Power
1. Connect the USB cable to a USB port.
2. Flip **POWER** up to ON. The display shows the set temperature, `100C`, and the heater ring glows white.
3. Every time the kettle is switched on, the set temperature starts at 100 °C and keep-warm starts off.
4. Flip **POWER** down to switch off. The display and the ring go dark.

## Filling
1. Lift the lid by its lip. It stays open on its own.
2. Fill the jug.
3. Lower the lid until it seats flat on the jug. It closes under its own weight.

## Setting the temperature
- Turn **TEMP** clockwise to raise the set temperature and anticlockwise to lower it.
- Each click of the dial changes it by 5 °C, from 60 °C to 100 °C. Turning past either end has no further effect.
- The display shows the setting, for example `85C`.

## Keep-warm
- Press **WARM** to switch keep-warm on. The display shows `WARM` after the set temperature, for example `85C WARM`.
- Press **WARM** again to switch it off.
- With keep-warm on, the kettle holds the water at the set temperature after it heats, until you switch keep-warm off, start another heat, or switch off.

## Heating
1. Close the lid. The kettle will not heat with the lid open.
2. Press **BOIL**. The display shows `BOILING` and the ring turns red. Heating takes about 8 seconds.
3. Press **BOIL** again at any time to stop heating.
4. When the water reaches the set temperature:
   - with keep-warm off, the display shows `DONE` and the ring turns green. Lifting the lid returns to the set temperature display.
   - with keep-warm on, the display shows `WARM` and the set temperature, for example `WARM 85C`, and the ring turns orange. Press **WARM** to stop holding.

## Display messages
| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `100C` | Ready; the set temperature | Press BOIL to heat |
| `BOILING` | Heating | Wait, or press BOIL to stop |
| `DONE` | Heated to the set temperature | Pour, or lift the lid to refill |
| `WARM 85C` | Holding at the set temperature | Press WARM to stop holding |
| `LID` | BOIL was pressed with the lid open | Close the lid, then press BOIL |

## Care
Never heat an empty jug. Wipe the base with a dry cloth and keep it out of water. Do not lift the jug off the base while heating.
