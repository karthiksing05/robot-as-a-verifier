# IT-90 Lab Interval Timer — User Manual

**Read this page before first use.**

## Parts
1. Display 2. +MIN button 3. +SEC button 4. START/STOP button 5. POWER switch 6. Status light (on top)

The controls are on the sloped front panel, left to right: +MIN, +SEC, START/STOP, POWER.

## Power
1. Connect the USB cable to a USB port.
2. Flip **POWER** up to ON. The display shows `00:00` and the status light stays off.
3. Flip **POWER** down to switch off. Switching off clears the set time.

## Setting the time
The display shows the time as minutes and seconds, `MM:SS`, from `00:00` to `99:59`.

1. Press **+MIN** to add 1 minute.
2. Press **+SEC** to add 1 second. Seconds carry into minutes: 59 seconds plus one press of +SEC shows `01:00`.
3. Presses past `99:59` are ignored.

Set the time while the timer is stopped. +MIN and +SEC do nothing while the timer is counting down or paused. To start again from `00:00`, switch the timer off and on.

## Running
1. Press **START/STOP** to start. The status light turns blue and the display counts down one second at a time.
2. Press **START/STOP** while it is counting to pause. The countdown holds at the time shown and the status light goes out.
3. Press **START/STOP** again to resume from the time shown.

With `00:00` set, START/STOP does nothing.

## When the time is up
The display shows `DONE` and the status light turns green. Press **START/STOP** to clear it: the display shows the last time you set, ready to run again.

## Display messages
| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `MM:SS` (for example `12:30`) | The time set, the time left, or the time held while paused | Nothing: set, start or resume as needed |
| `DONE` | The countdown has reached zero | Press START/STOP to clear |
| (blank) | The timer is switched off | Flip POWER up to ON |

## Care
Keep liquids away from the unit. Wipe with a dry cloth. Press the buttons with a fingertip; do not use tools or sharp objects.
