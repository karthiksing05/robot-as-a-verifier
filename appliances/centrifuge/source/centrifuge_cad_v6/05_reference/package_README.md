## Faults and the swap

- **F1, Stop button.** Printed bayonet bezel and captive printed cap. Rev A comes in 0.05 and 0.15 mm/side (keep the one that binds every time); Rev B is 0.40. To swap: twist 45° using the flange grip slots, pull it out with its cap, push the new one in, twist to lock.
- **F2, lid handle.** Laser 3-ply laminate with two J-tabs through slots in the lid. Rev A has a 3 mm upright pinch fin; Rev B has a hook lip (10 mm overhang, 10.9 mm clear underneath). To swap: slide 3 mm forward, lift, drop the other in, slide back.
- **F3, well A.** Printed bayonet insert, ID 15.6 (Rev A) vs 16.8 (Rev B). The stuck tube sits 8.5 mm high with its nub 6.0 mm above the beam. The lid still closes (3.0 mm), so Start shows `TUBE`.

## Checks

- **No two solids share any volume** (1e-6 mm³) in Rev A, Rev B, or any of the 8 Rev A / Rev B mixes, including the hinge pins and the electronics placeholders.
- **Motion and assembly paths: 25/25 pass** (`feasibility.json`):
  - lid and rack cover through 0–105°, stopping at 105°
  - knob through 270°
  - Stop bayonet twist and pull-out
  - well A twist and lift-out
  - handle slide-unlock and lift-off

- Gaps: the laser caps clear their holes by 0.4 mm. The lid fin clears the sensor body by 0.5 mm and sits 2 mm below the beam. The tube nub sits 2.5 mm below its beam. There are 6.0 mm under the tube sensor for its leads.
- Printed walls ≥ 1.2 mm. Every printed part is one valid solid on a 220 mm bed; every laser part is a closed profile.
- The model rebuilds cleanly at 2.7 / 3.2 mm sheet and 0.1 / 0.2 mm kerf.
