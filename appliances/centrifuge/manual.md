# MC-6 Mini Centrifuge — User Manual

**Read this page before first use.**

## Parts
1. Lid (clear) 2. Rotor with wells A and B 3. Display 4. POWER switch 5. START 6. STOP 7. TIME+ 8. SPEED dial 9. Tube rack with cover

## Power
Connect the USB cable to a USB port. Flip **POWER** up to ON. The display shows the set speed and the light ring glows white. Flip it down to switch off.

## Loading a tube
1. Lift the rack cover by its lip. It stays open on its own.
2. Lift a tube by its collar or its top loop.
3. Lift the centrifuge lid by its lip until it rests open.
4. Lower the tube into **well A** until the collar sits on the well. Do not force it.
5. Close the lid. It closes under its own weight.

Use only MC-6 tubes. Load well B for balance when running two samples.

## Setting speed and time
- Turn **SPEED** clockwise to raise the speed (1000 to 6000 rpm). The display shows the setting.
- Press **TIME+** to add 10 seconds. The display shows the run time.

## Running
- Press **START**. The ring shows the spin and the display counts down.
- Press **STOP** at any time to end the run.
- When the run ends the ring turns green. Wait for it before opening the lid.

## Display messages
| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `LID` | The lid is open | Close the lid, then press START |
| `TUBE` | No tube is seated in well A | Seat a tube in well A, then press START |

## Care
Keep liquids away from the unit. Wipe with a dry cloth. Do not place objects on the lid.
