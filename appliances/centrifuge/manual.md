# MC-6 Mini Centrifuge — User Manual

Model MC-6, with MC-R4 tube rack. Rev 1.0, November 2026.

## Quick start
1. Connect the USB cable. Flip **POWER** up to ON. The light ring glows white.
2. Lift the rack cover by its front lip. Lift a tube by its collar or top loop.
3. Lift the centrifuge lid by its front lip. Lower the tube into **well A** until the collar rests on the well.
4. Close the lid. Turn **SPEED** to set the speed; press **TIME+** to add 10 s.
5. Press **START**. Wait for the ring to turn green before opening the lid.

## Safety
- **WARNING:** Do not open the lid while the light ring shows a run. Press STOP and wait until the ring turns green.
- **WARNING:** When running two tubes, load wells A and B with tubes of the same type and fill level.
- **CAUTION:** Pinch point. Keep fingers clear of the hinge at the back of the lid and the rack cover when closing them.
- **CAUTION:** Don't move or bump the unit during a run. Clamp it to the bench by the rear base lip if a robot operates it.
- **NOTICE:** Keep liquids away from the housing and the control panel.
- **NOTICE:** Use only MC-6 tubes, MC-R4 racks and MC Instruments replacement parts.
- Use indoors on a firm, level surface. Power only from a 5 V USB port rated 500 mA or more. Never force a tube into a well.

### Control panel (left to right)
| Key | Control | Use |
|---|---|---|
| A | POWER switch | Up = ON, down = OFF. |
| B | START | Starts a run with the speed and time shown. |
| C | STOP | Ends a run at any time. When idle, resets the time to 30 s. |
| D | TIME+ | Adds 10 s per press, up to 10 min. |
| E | SPEED dial | Clockwise raises the speed: 1 = 1000 rpm, 6 = 6000 rpm. |

## Installation
1. Unpack the centrifuge, rack, tubes and USB cable.
2. Place the centrifuge on a level bench, control panel facing the operator. Leave 10 cm free behind it so the lid can open fully.
3. For robot operation, clamp the rear base lip to the bench and place the rack within reach.
4. Connect the USB cable at the back. Flip POWER up. The display shows the speed and time; the ring glows white.

Note: settings return to 1000 rpm and 30 s each time the unit is switched on.

## Operation
1. Lift the rack cover by its front lip until it rests open.
2. Lift the centrifuge lid by its front lip until it rests open past vertical.
3. Take a tube by its collar flats or top loop. Lower it straight down into well A until the collar rests on the well. A correct tube drops in by its own weight; don't press it.
4. For two samples, load well B with a matched tube.
5. Lower the lid. It closes under its own weight.
6. Turn SPEED and press TIME+ to set the run.
7. Press START. The ring shows the rotor turning and the display counts down. STOP ends the run early.
8. When the time runs out the ring turns green and the display shows DONE. Open the lid, unload, return the tube to the rack and close the cover.

If START is refused, the ring turns red and the display shows the reason. Fix it and press START again.

## Display and light ring
| Display | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `1000 RPM 0:30` | Ready. Set speed and time. | Load, close the lid, press START. |
| `4500 RPM 0:12` | Run in progress, time remaining. | Wait, or press STOP. |
| `DONE` | Run complete. | Open the lid and unload. |
| `LID` | The lid is open or not fully closed. | Lower the lid fully, then press START. |
| `TUBE` | No tube is seated in well A. | Seat a tube so its collar rests on the well, then press START. |

| Light ring | Status |
|---|---|
| Off | Power off. |
| White, steady | Ready. |
| Blue, rotating | Run in progress. |
| Green, steady | Run complete. The lid may be opened. |
| Red, steady | START refused, or the lid was opened during a run; see the display. |

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | Possible cause | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Display and ring stay dark | POWER is off, or no USB power. | Flip POWER up. Check the cable; use a 5 V port rated 500 mA or more. |
| `LID` after START | The lid is not fully down. | Lower the lid until it rests flat. |
| `TUBE` after START | No tube in well A, or the tube is sitting high. | Lift the tube and lower it again so the collar rests on the well. |
| Tube will not drop into a well | Wrong tube, debris, or a worn well insert. | Use MC-6 tubes. Check for debris. Replace the well A insert. |
| A button does not respond | POWER is off, or a loose wire. | Flip POWER up. If one button still does nothing, contact service. |
| Lid or cover won't stay open | Not opened fully. | Lift it past vertical until it rests on its stop. |
| Lid is hard to open | Lid lip damaged or missing. | Replace the lid lip. |
| Speed does not change | Dial at the end of its travel. | Turn the dial the other way. |

## Care
- Switch off and unplug before cleaning. Wipe with a soft cloth, dry or lightly dampened with 70 % ethanol. Never spray liquid on the unit.
- **Well A insert:** turn 45° clockwise (seen from above) and lift out. Fit the new one with its tabs in the notches and turn 45° anticlockwise to lock.
- **Lid lip:** slide 3 mm toward the front of the lid and lift off. Fit by pushing its tabs down through the lid slots and sliding 3 mm toward the hinge.
