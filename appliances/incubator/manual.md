# MI-45 Mini Incubator — User Manual

**Read this page before first use.**

## Parts
1. Chamber door (hinged at the back, with a window) 2. Door pull lip 3. Chamber with shelf 4. POWER switch 5. TEMP− 6. TEMP+ 7. Display 8. Status light

## Power
Connect the USB cable to a USB port. Flip **POWER** up to ON. The display shows the set temperature, `SET 37.0C`, and the status light glows green. The set temperature returns to 37.0 °C each time you switch on. Flip **POWER** down to switch off.

## Setting the temperature
1. Press **TEMP+** to raise the set temperature by 0.5 °C.
2. Press **TEMP−** to lower it by 0.5 °C.
3. The range is 25.0 to 45.0 °C. At either end, further presses are ignored.

The display shows the setting, for example `SET 37.5C`.

## Opening and closing the door
1. Hook a finger under the pull lip at the front edge of the door and lift.
2. The door opens upward to 90° and rests open on its own.
3. To close, draw the door back past upright and let it fall. It closes under its own weight.

## Door alarm
Keep door openings short. If the door stays open for 5 seconds, the display shows `DOOR` and the status light turns red. Close the door to clear the alarm. The display returns to the set temperature and the light to green.

## Display messages
| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `SET 37.0C` | The set temperature | Press TEMP+ or TEMP− to change it |
| `DOOR` | The door has been open 5 seconds or more | Close the door |

## Care
Wipe the chamber with a dry cloth. Do not place objects on the door. Keep the vents at the back clear.
