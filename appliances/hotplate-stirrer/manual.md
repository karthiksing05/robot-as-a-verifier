# HPS-300 Hotplate Stirrer — User Manual

**Read this page before first use. The plate gets hot.**

## Parts
1. Hot plate with vessel cradle 2. POWER switch 3. HEAT dial 4. STIR dial 5. Display 6. HOT light 7. Beaker rest 8. 15 mL conical beaker with grip collar and lifting loop

## Power
Connect the USB cable. Flip **POWER** up to ON. The display shows the HEAT and STIR settings. Flip it down to switch off.

## Loading the beaker
1. Lift the beaker from the beaker rest by its collar or its lifting loop.
2. Lower it into the cradle in the centre of the plate until the collar sits on the cradle. Do not force it.

The plate heats and stirs only while a beaker is seated in the cradle. To unload, turn both dials to 0 and lift the beaker straight out.

## Setting heat and stirring
- Turn **HEAT** clockwise to raise the plate temperature, from 0 (off) to 300 °C in steps of 25 °C. Turn it fully anticlockwise to 0 to stop heating.
- Turn **STIR** clockwise to raise the stirring speed, from 0 (off) to 1500 rpm in steps of 100 rpm. Turn it fully anticlockwise to 0 to stop stirring.
- The display shows the two settings, for example `150C 600RPM`. Heating and stirring start as soon as a dial leaves 0, and follow the dials while they run.

## HOT light
- The red **HOT** light comes on when the plate starts heating.
- It stays lit after HEAT is turned back to 0, and after the unit is switched off, until the plate has cooled. Do not touch the plate or move the unit while it is lit.
- If you switch on again while the plate is still hot, the HOT light stays lit.

## Display messages
| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `150C 600RPM` | The HEAT and STIR settings | — |
| `NO VESSEL` | A dial is above 0 but no beaker is seated in the cradle | Seat the beaker in the cradle, or turn both dials to 0 |
| (blank, HOT lit) | Switched off, plate still hot | Wait for the HOT light to go out |

## Care
Use only HPS beakers. Keep the plate clean and dry. Wipe with a damp cloth only when the HOT light is out and the unit is unplugged. Do not leave the unit heating unattended.
