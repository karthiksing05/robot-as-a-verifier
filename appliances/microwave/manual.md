# MW-1 Mock Microwave — User Manual

Rev 1.0, November 2026.

## Quick start
1. Plug the USB cable into the back. The display lights white and shows **READY**.
2. Pull the handle to open the door. Put the item inside. Push the door shut until it clicks.
3. Press **TIME** to set the cook time. Press **POWER** to choose HI, MED or LO.
4. Press **START**. The display turns amber and counts down.
5. At the end the display shows **DONE** and turns green. Open the door.

Shortcut: press **+30s** at any time to add 30 seconds. If the oven is idle it starts at once on HI.

## Safety
- **NOTICE:** The MW-1 is a mock-up. It has no magnetron and does not heat. Never put food you intend to eat, liquids or metal inside.
- **CAUTION:** Keep fingers clear of the hinge side when closing the door. It can pinch.
- Use the USB supply only (5 V DC, 0.5 A or more). There is no mains wiring.
- Don't hang anything from the open door. Keep the vents on the right side clear.

## Installation
1. Place the MW-1 on a flat, level surface. Leave 30 mm clear on the right side for the vents.
2. Leave the area in front clear so the door can swing fully open to the left.
3. Connect the USB cable at the back to a USB port or 5 V adapter.
4. The display shows READY with a white backlight.

## Operation
**Door:** grip the handle and pull straight toward you. There's light resistance, then a click as the latch lets go. Swing the door open to the left. To close, push it shut near the handle until it clicks. The oven won't start or keep running while the door is open.

**Cooking:**
1. Close the door.
2. Press TIME until the display shows the time you want.
3. Press POWER to choose HI, MED or LO. The default is HI.
4. Press START. The backlight turns amber and the time counts down.
5. To pause, press STOP or open the door. Press START to resume.
6. At 0:00 the display shows DONE and turns green for 10 s, then returns to READY.

**Sounds:**
| Sound | When |
|---|---|
| One short beep | Any button is pressed. |
| Three beeps | Cooking has finished (DONE). |
| Two low beeps | START was refused: the door is open or no time is set. |

## Display messages

| READY | White | Idle, no time set. |
| COOK m:ss / POWER xx | White | Time and power set, waiting for START. |
| m:ss counting down | Amber | Cooking. |
| PAUSED | Amber | STOP pressed during cooking. START resumes, STOP clears. |
| DOOR OPEN | Red | Door opened during cooking. Close it and press START to resume. |
| DONE | Green | Cook finished. |
| SET TIME | White | START pressed with no time set. |
| CLOSE DOOR | Red | START pressed with the door open. |

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | Check |
|---|---|
| Display blank | USB cable seated? Try another port. Check the display cable inside the electronics bay. |
| Display lit but no text | Display contrast or wiring. The display needs 5 V. |
| START does nothing | Is the door fully shut (you heard the click)? Is a time set? |
| Door won't stay shut | The latch arm may be bent or the catch loose. See Care. |
| No beep when a button is pressed | The buzzer cable in the electronics bay may be loose. The oven still works. |
| One button does nothing | Check its two wires behind the control panel. |

## Care
- Wipe with a dry or slightly damp cloth. No solvents on the acrylic window.
- Don't oil the hinges.
- If the door no longer clicks shut, the printed latch on the shelf inside can be replaced. It is glued in place.
