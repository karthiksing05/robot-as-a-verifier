# HT-20 Room Thermostat — User Manual

**Model HT-20 with DS-1 desk stand. Read this page before first use.**

## Parts
1. Display 2. Status light (top edge) 3. MODE button 4. DOWN button (▼) 5. UP button (▲) 6. Desk stand

The three buttons sit in a row along the top of the face, above the display. Seen from the front, left to right: **MODE**, **DOWN**, **UP**.

## Power
The HT-20 has no power switch. It is on whenever its USB cable is connected.

At power-up the thermostat always starts in **OFF** with the setpoint at **20.0 °C**. Settings are not kept when the power is removed. The display shows `OFF 20.0C` and the status light is dark.

## Choosing a mode
Press **MODE** to step through the modes, one step per press:

**OFF** → **HEAT** → **COOL** → **OFF** …

| Mode | What it does | Status light |
|---|---|---|
| OFF | No heating or cooling | off |
| HEAT | Heats the room up to the setpoint | orange |
| COOL | Cools the room down to the setpoint | blue |

## Setting the temperature
1. Press **UP** to raise the setpoint by 0.5 °C.
2. Press **DOWN** to lower the setpoint by 0.5 °C.
3. The setpoint runs from **10.0 °C** to **30.0 °C**. At either end, a further press in that direction does nothing: the display stays at the limit.

The buttons set the temperature in every mode, OFF included. Each press is one step. The display shows the new setpoint at once.

## The display
The display always shows the mode and the setpoint, in degrees Celsius, to one decimal place:

`HEAT 21.5C` means heating, setpoint 21.5 °C.

## Display messages
| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `OFF 20.0C` | Off, setpoint 20.0 °C | Press MODE to heat or cool |
| `HEAT 21.5C` | Heating to 21.5 °C | Press UP or DOWN to change the setpoint |
| `COOL 24.0C` | Cooling to 24.0 °C | Press UP or DOWN to change the setpoint |

## Care
Mount or stand the thermostat away from heaters, lamps and direct sun. Wipe with a dry cloth. Do not press the buttons with anything sharp.
