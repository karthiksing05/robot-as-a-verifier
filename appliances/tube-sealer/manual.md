# HS-20 Tube Heat Sealer — User Manual

**Read this page before first use.**

The HS-20 heat-seals the cap of one sample tube at a time. It comes with a one-tube stand.

## Parts
1. Sealing slot (top of the unit) 2. Display 3. Status light 4. CLAMP lever 5. SEAL button 6. Tube stand

## Power
Connect the USB cable to a USB port. The unit is on whenever it is connected: there is no power switch. The display shows `READY` and the status light glows white.

## Loading a tube
1. Lift the tube from the stand by its collar or its top loop.
2. Lower it straight down into the **sealing slot** until the collar rests on the slot's rim. Do not force it. The unit senses a seated tube.
3. Flip **CLAMP** up to close the sealing jaws on the tube. The lever stays up.

Use only HS-20 tubes (16 mm body, 22 mm collar).

## Sealing
1. Check that a tube is seated and **CLAMP** is up.
2. Press **SEAL** once. The display shows `SEALING` with the seconds left, and the status light turns amber.
3. Sealing takes 5 seconds. When it ends, the display shows `DONE` and the light turns green.
4. Flip **CLAMP** down to open the jaws. The display returns to `READY`. Lift the tube out by its collar and return it to the stand.

SEAL does nothing to a tube unless both conditions hold: it refuses with a message instead (see below). Flipping **CLAMP** down during sealing stops the seal.

## Display messages
| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `READY` | Waiting for a tube | Load a tube and close the clamp |
| `NO TUBE` | SEAL was pressed with no tube seated in the slot | Seat a tube, then press SEAL |
| `CLAMP` | SEAL was pressed with the clamp open, or the clamp was opened during sealing | Flip CLAMP up, then press SEAL |
| `SEALING` | Sealing, with the seconds left | Wait. Do not open the clamp |
| `DONE` | The seal is finished | Flip CLAMP down and remove the tube |

The status light is red while `NO TUBE` or `CLAMP` shows.

## Care
The jaws are warm after sealing: wait a few seconds before touching the tube cap. Keep the slot free of debris. Wipe with a dry cloth.
