# CR-600 Cuvette Photometer — User Manual

Single-wavelength (600 nm) absorbance reader for standard 12.5 mm square cuvettes. **Read this page before first use.**

## Parts
1. Sample well with the chamber slot 2. Lid with lip 3. Status light 4. Display 5. POWER switch 6. BLANK 7. READ 8. Cuvette holder (side of the unit) 9. Cuvette, 12.5 mm square, 45 mm tall

## Power
Connect the USB cable to a USB port. Flip **POWER** up to ON. The display shows `READY` and the status light glows green. Flip it down to switch off. Switching off clears the blank.

## Loading a cuvette
1. Lift the cuvette straight up out of the side holder, holding it by its upper end.
2. Lift the lid by its lip until it rests open behind the well.
3. Lower the cuvette straight into the chamber slot in the floor of the well until it stands on the bottom. Its top stays about 13 mm above the well floor, just below the deck. Do not force it.
4. Close the lid. It closes under its own weight.

The lid must be closed for every measurement. Return the cuvette to the side holder when you are done.

## Blanking
Blank once each session, before the first reading, with a cuvette of the solvent or buffer alone.
1. Load the blank cuvette and close the lid.
2. Press **BLANK**. The display shows `BLANKING` and the light turns blue for about 2 seconds.
3. The display shows `BLANK OK` and the light turns green. The blank holds until the photometer is switched off.

## Reading a sample
1. Load the sample cuvette and close the lid.
2. Press **READ**. The display shows `READING` and the light turns blue for about 2 seconds.
3. The display shows the absorbance, for example `A=0.42`, and the light turns green. Press **READ** again to repeat the reading.

## Display messages
| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `READY` | Switched on, not yet blanked | Blank before reading |
| `BLANK OK` | The blank is stored | Load a sample, then press READ |
| `A=0.42` | Absorbance of the sample at 600 nm | Record the value |
| `BLANK FIRST` | READ was pressed before a blank this session | Load a blank, press BLANK, then press READ |
| `LID` | The lid is open | Close the lid, then press the button again |
| `NO SAMPLE` | No cuvette is seated in the chamber | Seat a cuvette, then press the button again |

The light glows red while `BLANK FIRST`, `LID` or `NO SAMPLE` is shown.

## Care
Keep the well and chamber dry and dust-free. Wipe spills at once with a dry cloth. Handle cuvettes by their upper end, never by the clear faces. Keep the lid closed when not in use.
