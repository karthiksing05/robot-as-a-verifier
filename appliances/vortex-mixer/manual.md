# VM-3 Touch Vortex Mixer — User Manual

**Read this page before first use.**

## Parts
1. Cup head (on top) 2. Sample cup 3. Cup holder (right side) 4. Display 5. MODE switch 6. START 7. SPEED dial

## Power
Connect the USB cable to a USB port. The mixer is on whenever it has power: there is no power switch. The display shows the mode and the set speed.

## Choosing a mode
The **MODE** switch stays where you put it.
- **Up: TOUCH.** The mixer runs while a cup sits in the cup head, and stops when you lift the cup out. START is not used.
- **Down: ON.** Press START to begin mixing and press it again to stop.

The display shows the mode: `TOUCH` or `ON`, then the set speed.

## Setting the speed
- Turn **SPEED** clockwise to raise the speed, from 500 rpm (fully anticlockwise) to 3000 rpm (fully clockwise), in steps of 100 rpm.
- When the mixer is not running, the display shows the set speed after the mode, for example `ON 1500 RPM`.
- While it is mixing, the display shows the head's speed, for example `1500 RPM`, and turning SPEED changes it at once.

## Loading a cup
1. Lift the sample cup out of the holder by its cap or its top loop.
2. Lower it straight into the cup head until the cap rests on the head. Do not force it.
3. To unload, lift the cup straight up by its cap and return it to the holder.

Use only VM-3 sample cups.

## Mixing
**ON mode (switch down)**
1. Load a cup.
2. Press **START**. The head spins and the display shows its speed.
3. Press **START** again to stop. The display goes back to the mode and the set speed.

**TOUCH mode (switch up)**
1. Set the speed first.
2. Load a cup. Mixing starts as soon as the cup is seated.
3. Lift the cup out to stop.

## Display messages
| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `ON 1500 RPM` | ON mode, ready, set to 1500 rpm | Load a cup and press START |
| `TOUCH 1500 RPM` | TOUCH mode, waiting for a cup, set to 1500 rpm | Load a cup to mix |
| `1500 RPM` | Mixing at 1500 rpm | Press START (ON mode) or lift the cup (TOUCH mode) to stop |
| `CUP` | START was pressed with no cup seated in the head | Seat a cup in the cup head, then press START |

## Care
Keep liquids off the panel. Wipe with a dry cloth. Do not run the mixer with anything but a VM-3 cup in the head.
